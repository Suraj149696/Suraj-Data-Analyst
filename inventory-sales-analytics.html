Business Insights

What the data revealed

Key findings across inventory, sales, vendors, and profitability.

Strengths
  • $452M annual revenue with 13% monthly growth rate
  • Turnover ratio 7.23 — healthy stock movement
  • Premium spirits (whiskey, vodka, rum) drive consistent profitability
  • 5 Power BI dashboards enable real-time decision-making
Challenges
  • 1,944 loss-making SKUs dragging down overall margins
  • Dead stock accumulation increasing holding costs
  • Vendor concentration — top 5 dominate procurement spend
  • Store 81 operates at a net loss
Strategic Recommendations
01 · Inventory
ABC-based reorder points
Dynamic restocking rules — liquidate dead stock immediately
02 · Products
Reprice or discontinue
Eliminate 1,944 loss-making SKUs; double down on top 20%
03 · Vendors
Diversify supply base
Reduce DIAGEO dependency; renegotiate freight contracts
04 · Stores
Benchmark underperformers
Align Store 81 inventory with Store 76's successful model
05 · Forecasting
Deploy ARIMA / Prophet
Seasonal demand prediction to pre-empt Q4 stockouts
06 · Analytics
Automated margin alerts
SKU-level profit forecasting embedded in Power BI
